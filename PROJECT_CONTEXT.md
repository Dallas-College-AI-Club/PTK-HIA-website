## How We Work

### Meetings

- **Weekly Teams check-in with PTK:** Fridays, same time as the kickoff
- **In-person AI club work sessions:** likely Tuesdays, or whenever works for most people. PTK is welcome to join.
- **AI club regular meeting:** Thursdays at 2:30
- **Group chat:** day-to-day communication. PTK can add anyone who wants to help.

### Roles

- The **PTK President** is the first person to go to for yes/no decisions and for a second set of eyes or opinions.
- **PTK testing:** The PTK President forwards each new build to PTK officers for feedback on design, conversation flow, and languages.
- For AI club roles, see [Team](README.md#team) in the README.

### Documentation

- The AI club documents technical work in plain language so PTK can use and maintain the site in the future.
- PTK sends resource info to the AI club, and the AI club adds it to GitHub.
